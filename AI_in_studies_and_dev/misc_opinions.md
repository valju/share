# AI use in studying and SW development

## General principles

* Be in control, always use a maximum effort trying to stay on the wagon and leash
* But then be curious and utilize AI to the fullest
* SW developer is finally still responsible for the output, so audit and control the quality
* AI tools (using LLM + control code) are based on information mass, they are great when solving something that has been done a lot of times. 
* And might fail in some new frontier stuff badly
* On the otherhand in some tasks they are a lot faster than multitude of people, so the excel in going through a lot of options, combinations and patterns. (E.g. suggest new chemicals, based on properties of existing ones, but then there will be hazardous mistakes too)  

## Studies

* Think fast yourself, maybe even brainstorm your own ideas first
* Only then ask AI
* And third be critical and challenge the generated answers and knowledge
